# Unit 6 Vocabulary

## Class 31: Legendary Commercials

### Class Profile

| Vocabulary | Can-do Statement |
| --- | --- |
| Common marketing and advertising verb phrases | Talk about commercials and marketing. |

| No. | Word / Phrase | Definition |
| ---: | --- | --- |
| 1 | be persuasive | To be able to make people agree to do something; convincing. |
| 2 | capture one's attention | To interest someone; to grab their attention. |
| 3 | encourage brand loyalty | To make customers really like a brand and want to keep buying its products. |
| 4 | endorse | To make a public statement of approval or support for something or someone. |
| 5 | expose one's competitive advantage | To share the special or unique things that make a product better than others in the same field. |
| 6 | focus on an up-market group | To be designed for consumers with high incomes. |
| 7 | generate a lot of buzz | To draw positive attention and interest in something; to create interest in a product or service. |
| 8 | go viral | To become very popular or well known by being published on the internet. |
| 9 | identify the target audience | To find the particular group of people that an advertisement or product is aimed at. |
| 10 | influence buying habits | To affect customers' decisions about the products they usually buy. |
| 11 | raise awareness | To inform and educate people about a topic or issue with the intention of influencing their attitude. |
