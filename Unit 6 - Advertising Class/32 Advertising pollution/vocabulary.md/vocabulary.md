## Advertising Vocabulary

| No. | Word / Phrase | Definition |
| ---: | --- | --- |
| 1 | advertising space | Space on a website, billboard, in the press, etc. that people pay for to advertise something. |
| 2 | bargain | Something on sale at a lower price than its true value. |
| 3 | brand exposure | The actions taken to ensure a brand is seen, noticed, and recognized by consumers and audiences. |
| 4 | catchy slogan | An attractive and easy-to-remember motto. |
| 5 | digital screen | A flat vertical surface on which pictures or words are shown. |
| 6 | discount | The difference between the original price and the lower price something is being sold at. |
| 7 | mom-and-pop shop | A small retail store owned and operated by members of the same family. |
| 8 | neon sign | A sign illuminated by neon or neon lamps. |
| 9 | outdoor advertising | Advertising that reaches consumers while they are outside their homes. |
| 10 | public perception | The way someone thinks and feels about a company, product, service, etc. |
| 11 | retailer | A person, shop, or business that sells goods to the public. |
| 12 | sales tactics | Strategies that can help sales professionals sign more customers and sell more products. |
| 13 | special offer | A product, service, or programme that is offered at a reduced price. |
| 14 | visual pollution | Elements that have a negative impact on the aesthetic quality of a landscape or view. |
| 15 | wholesaler | Someone who buys and sells goods in large amounts to shops and businesses. |
