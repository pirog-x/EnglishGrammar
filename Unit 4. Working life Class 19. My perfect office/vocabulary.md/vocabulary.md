## Workplace Nouns and Adjectives

| No. | Word / Phrase | Definition |
| ---: | --- | --- |
| 1 | custom-made desk | A desk that is made to the customer's own design or specification. |
| 2 | French window | A window that has two parts which open outwards like doors. |
| 3 | fully stocked place | A place with a lot of different things in it. |
| 4 | hot-desk system | A system in which people do not have their own desks but use any desk that is free. |
| 5 | pet-friendly office | A place that is suitable for pets or where you can stay with your pet. |
| 6 | self-catering cafeteria | A place where people can prepare and cook their own food. |
| 7 | state-of-the-art device | An object or machine that is very modern and uses the latest technology. |
| 8 | sophisticated device | A device or machine that is complex and advanced. |
| 9 | vending machine | A machine that sells food, drinks, cigarettes, etc. when you put coins into it. |

## Fully and Self- Adjectives

| No. | Word / Phrase | Definition |
| ---: | --- | --- |
| 1 | fully aware of | Having complete knowledge or understanding of something. |
| 2 | fully booked | When all available spaces or time slots have been reserved or taken. |
| 3 | fully informed | Possessing all the necessary information about a particular subject or situation. |
| 4 | fully integrated | Combined or merged completely to form a whole. |
| 5 | fully interactive | Allowing for two-way communication or interaction between people and technology. |
| 6 | fully loaded | Containing the maximum amount or quantity that can be held or carried. |
| 7 | fully refundable | Able to be returned for a full refund of the purchase price. |
| 8 | fully staffed | Having all the required personnel or employees in place to operate effectively. |
| 9 | self-centred | Excessively focused on oneself and one's own needs, often at the expense of others. |
| 10 | self-employed | Working for oneself rather than being employed by someone else. |
| 11 | self-proclaimed | Claiming a particular title or status without official recognition or authorization from others. |
| 12 | self-taught | Having acquired knowledge or skills through one's own efforts and resources rather than formal education or training. |
