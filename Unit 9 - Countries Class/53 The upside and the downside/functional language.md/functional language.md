# Unit 9 Functional Language

## Class 53: The Upside and the Downside

### Class Profile

| Functional language | Can-do Statement |
| --- | --- |
| Discussing pros and cons, contrasting, saying your opinion | Talk about advantages and disadvantages, use contrasting phrases, and express your opinion. |

### Word Pairs

| Word Pair | Definition | Example |
| --- | --- | --- |
| bed and breakfast | a room to sleep in for the night and a morning meal | We stayed in a cozy **bed and breakfast** on our trip to the countryside. |
| law and order | system of rules and enforcement maintaining societal control | The government promised to restore **law and order** after the protests. |
| sooner or later | at some time in the future | **Sooner or later**, we will have to make a decision about where to go for vacation. |
| ups and downs | positive and negative aspects | Living abroad has its **ups and downs**, but it teaches you a lot. |
| more or less | approximately | The journey takes **more or less** two hours by train. |
| hit and miss | inconsistent; you cannot depend on it to be of good quality, on time, accurate, etc. | His performance was **hit and miss** during the game, so he needs to improve. |

### Discussing Pros

| Phrase | Example |
| --- | --- |
| An argument for this is... | **An argument for** living in Paris **is** that it offers excellent career opportunities. |
| One advantage is... | **One advantage is** that the public transport system is reliable and easy to use. |
| It offers several benefits, such as... | **It offers several benefits, such as** access to world-class museums, art, and historical sites. |
| A positive aspect... | **A positive aspect** of living in Venice is the rich cultural heritage and artistic atmosphere. |
| An upside to this is... | **An upside to this is** that students can meet people from many different countries. |
| It's a plus that... | **It's a plus that** Paris provides access to a wide range of educational and career opportunities. |

### Discussing Cons

| Phrase | Example |
| --- | --- |
| One disadvantage is... | **One disadvantage is** that housing costs can be significantly higher. |
| An objection to... | **An objection to** moving abroad is that it can be difficult to leave family behind. |
| An argument against this is... | **An argument against** living in a big city **is** that the cost of living is often high. |
| It's a con that... | **It's a con that** the city center is very crowded during the tourist season. |
| A downside to this is... | **A downside to this is** that commuting can take a long time. |
| It offers several drawbacks, such as... | **It offers several drawbacks, such as** expensive rent and heavy traffic. |

### Contrasting

| Phrase | Example |
| --- | --- |
| Initially... | **Initially**, moving to a capital city can feel exciting and full of possibilities. |
| Conversely... | **Conversely**, smaller towns often offer a quieter and less stressful lifestyle. |
| While... | **While** Paris has excellent universities, it can be very expensive for students. |
| However... | The city has many job opportunities; **however**, competition can be intense. |
| Whereas... | **Whereas** Venice is famous for its beauty, Milan is better known for business and fashion. |
| Nonetheless... | Housing is expensive; **nonetheless**, many people still choose to live there. |
| In comparison... | **In comparison** with London, Warsaw is more affordable for students. |
| On the contrary... | This city is not boring; **on the contrary**, there is something interesting to do every weekend. |
| On the one hand... | **On the one hand**, the city's educational institutions are top-notch. |
| On the other hand... | **On the other hand**, housing costs can be significantly higher. |
| On one side of the coin... | **On one side of the coin**, living in a tourist city can bring many job opportunities. |
| On the flip side... | **On the flip side**, the streets can become overcrowded in summer. |

### Saying Your Opinion

| Phrase | Example |
| --- | --- |
| From my perspective, ... | **From my perspective**, it's a plus that Sydney enjoys a pleasant climate for most of the year. |
| From where I stand, ... | **From where I stand**, living abroad is worth the effort. |
| I'm inclined to think that ... | **I'm inclined to think that** smaller cities are better for families. |
| I feel that ... | **I feel that** good public transport makes city life much easier. |
| In my view... | **In my view**, cultural diversity is one of the biggest advantages of living in a large city. |
| It seems to me that ... | **It seems to me that** every country has its own ups and downs. |
