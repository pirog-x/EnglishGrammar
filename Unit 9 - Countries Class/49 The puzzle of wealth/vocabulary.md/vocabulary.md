# Unit 9 Vocabulary

## Class 49: The Puzzle of Wealth

### Class Profile

| Topic | Can-do Statement |
| --- | --- |
| Wealth, trade, and economic development | Use vocabulary to talk about wealth, prices, trade, imports, exports, and economic change. |

|  No. | Word / Phrase            | Definition                                                                                                                   |
| ---: | ------------------------ | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
|    1 | allocate                 | To give a particular amount of time, money, etc. to someone or something, so that it can be used in a particular way.        |
|    2 | determine                | To control or influence something directly.                                                                                  |
|    3 | drive the prices up      | To force prices to go up.                                                                                                    |
|    4 | grow wealthy             | To get rich.                                                                                                                 |
|    5 | have something in spades | To have something in large amounts or to a very great degree.                                                                |
|    6 | hinder                   | To make it difficult for something to develop or succeed.                                                                    |
|    7 | exceed                   | To be more than a particular number or amount.                                                                               |
|    8 | prosper                  | To grow and develop in a successful way, especially by becoming rich or making a large profit.                               |
|    9 | stand a chance           | To have a chance of success.                                                                                                 |
|   10 | stagnate                 | To stop developing or making progress.                                                                                       |
|   11 | conflict                 | A state of disagreement or argument between people, groups, or countries.                                                    |
|   12 | to conflict              | If two ideas, beliefs, opinions, etc. conflict, they cannot exist together or both be true.                                  |
|   13 | decrease                 | The process of becoming less, or the amount by which something becomes less.                                                 |
|   14 | to decrease              | To become less or go down to a lower level.                                                                                  |
|   15 | increase                 | A rise in amount, number, or degree.                                                                                         |
|   16 | to increase              | To become bigger in amount, number, or degree.                                                                               |
|   17 | export                   | A product that you sell in another country, or the business of sending goods to another country in order to sell them there. |
|   18 | to export                | To send goods to another country for sale.                                                                                   |
|   19 | import                   | Goods bought by one country from another; the action of bringing goods or fashions into a country.                           |
|   20 | to import                | To buy or bring in products from another country.                                                                            |
|   21 | project                  | A carefully planned piece of work to get information about something, build something, improve something, etc.               |
|   22 | to project               | To calculate what something will be in the future, using the information you have now.                                       |
|   23 | (the) produce            | Food or any other substance or material that is grown or obtained through farming.                                           |
|   24 | to produce               | To create something or bring something into existence.                                                                       |
|   25 | trade                    | The activity of buying, selling, or exchanging goods within a country or between countries.                                  |
|   26 | to trade                 | To buy and sell goods or services, especially between countries.                                                             |
|   27 | transport                | The process or business of taking goods from one place to another.                                                           |
|   28 | to transport             | To take goods or people from one place to another.                                                                           |
