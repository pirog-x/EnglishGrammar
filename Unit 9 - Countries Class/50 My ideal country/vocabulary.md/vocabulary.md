# Unit 9 Vocabulary

## Class 50: My Ideal Country

### Class Profile

| Topic | Can-do Statement |
| --- | --- |
| Landforms and describing places | Use landscape vocabulary and descriptive adjectives to describe an ideal country. |

|  No. | Word / Phrase  | Definition                                                                                                           |
| ---: | -------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
|    1 | coast          | The area where the land meets the sea.                                                                               |
|    2 | cliff          | A high area of rock with a very steep side, often on a coast.                                                        |
|    3 | glacier        | A large mass of ice that moves slowly.                                                                               |
|    4 | gorge          | A deep, narrow valley with steep sides, usually formed by a river or stream cutting through hard rock.               |
|    5 | harbour        | An area of water next to the coast, often protected from the sea by a thick wall, where ships and boats can shelter. |
|    6 | ice cap        | A thick layer of ice that permanently covers an area of land.                                                        |
|    7 | mountain range | A group or line of mountains with a particular name.                                                                 |
|    8 | ridge          | A long area of high land, especially at the top of a mountain.                                                       |
|    9 | slope          | A surface that lies at an angle to the horizontal, so that some points on it are higher than others.                 |
|   10 | shore          | The land along the edge of a sea, lake, or wide river.                                                               |
|   11 | summit         | The highest point of a mountain.                                                                                     |
|   12 | peninsula      | A long piece of land that sticks out from a larger area of land into the sea or into a lake.                         |
|   13 | valley         | An area of low land between hills or mountains, often with a river running through it.                               |
|   14 | blooming       | Flowering; thriving.                                                                                                 |
|   15 | diverse        | Very different from each other.                                                                                      |
|   16 | lush           | With abundant vegetation.                                                                                            |
|   17 | marvellous     | Extremely good, enjoyable, or impressive.                                                                            |
|   18 | picturesque    | Scenic; as if from a photograph.                                                                                     |
|   19 | pristine       | Extremely fresh or clean.                                                                                            |
|   20 | tranquil       | Pleasantly calm, quiet, and peaceful.                                                                                |
|   21 | vast           | Expansive; endless.                                                                                                  |
