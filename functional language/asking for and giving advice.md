# asking for and giving advice

# Asking for advice

- What can I do?
- What can you suggest?
- Any thoughts on this?
- What should I do?
- Have you got any ideas?
- What would you do?

# Giving advice

- If I were you, I’d...
- You had better / ought to...
- My advice would be...
- I think you should...
- How / what about ...?
- Why don’t you ...?
