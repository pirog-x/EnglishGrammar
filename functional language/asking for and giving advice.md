# asking for and giving advice

# Asking for advice

- What can I do?
- What can you suggest?
- Any thoughts on this?
- What should I do?
- Have you got any ideas?
- What would you do?

# Giving advice

- If I were you, I’d...
- You had better / ought to...
- My advice would be...
- I think you should...
- How / what about ... + noun or gerund?
- Why don’t you ...?

| Expression            | Verb Form                          | Example                                                                      |
|-----------------------|------------------------------------|------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| If I were you, I'd... | base verb (after "would")          | If I were you, I'd **talk** to her.                                          |
| You had better...     | base verb (without "to")           | You had better **leave** now.                                                |
| You ought to...       | to-infinitive                      | You ought **to see** a doctor.                                               |
| My advice would be... | to-infinitive                      | My advice would be **to wait**.                                              |
| I think you should... | base verb (after modal "should")   | I think you should **apologize**.                                            |
| How / What about...?  | gerund (-ing)                      | How about **trying** a new approach?                                         |
| Why don't you...?     | base verb                          | Why don't you **call** him?                                                  |
| I suggest (that)...   | base verb (subjunctive) OR gerund  | I suggest you **see** a specialist. / I suggest **seeing** a specialist.     |
| I recommend (that)... | base verb (subjunctive) OR gerund  | I recommend you **take** a break. / I recommend **taking** a break.          |
| I advise...           | gerund                             | I advise **waiting**.                                                        |
| I advise you to do sth...           | object + to-infinitive             | I advise you **to wait**.                                                    |

> I advise **...** an emergency kit with essentials in case of severe weather.

_having_

Explanation:
The verb "advise" can be followed by two different structures:

- advise + gerund (-ing) - when giving general advice

    I advise **having** an emergency kit.

- advise + object + to-infinitive - when advising a specific person

    I advise **you to have** an emergency kit.

---

> I suggest she **...** mindfulness exercises into her daily routine to reduce stress.

incorporate

### Explanation:
The verb "suggest" can be followed by different structures:

- suggest + (that) + subject + base verb - subjunctive form (formal)

    _I suggest (that) she incorporate..._

- suggest + gerund (-ing) - for general suggestions

    _I suggest incorporating mindfulness exercises._

Since this sentence has a subject clause "she," we use the subjunctive form, which requires the base verb "incorporate" (without 's').

- suggest
- recommend
- propose
- demand
- insist
- request

The pattern is: **suggest + (that) + subject + base verb**
