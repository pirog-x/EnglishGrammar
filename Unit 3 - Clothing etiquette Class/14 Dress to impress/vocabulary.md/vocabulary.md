# Unit 3 Vocabulary

## Class 14: Dress to Impress

### Class Profile

| Vocabulary | Can-do Statement |
| --- | --- |
| Adjectives and phrasal verbs to speak about clothing | Describe clothes and talk about the way people dress. |

## Clothing Adjectives

| No. | Word | Definition |
| ---: | --- | --- |
| 1 | artificial | Made by people, often as a copy of something natural. |
| 2 | brand-new | Very new; never used before. |
| 3 | loose | Not fitting tightly; not fastened securely. |
| 4 | neat | Tidy and clean. |
| 5 | pin-striped | Having thin lines that are close together. |
| 6 | polka-dotted | Having a pattern of small round spots. |
| 7 | old-fashioned | Not modern. |
| 8 | short-sleeved | Having short sleeves. |
| 9 | scruffy | Untidy and looking a little dirty. |
| 10 | torn | Worn or damaged in parts. |

## Clothing Phrasal Verbs

| No. | Phrasal Verb | Definition |
| ---: | --- | --- |
| 1 | dress up | To dress yourself or another person in your best clothes; to put on special clothes for special occasions. |
| 2 | put on | To dress yourself or someone else with an item or items of clothing. |
| 3 | wrap yourself up | To dress in warm clothes. |
| 4 | do up | To fasten something. |
| 5 | try on | To put on a piece of clothing to see if it fits you. |
| 6 | zip up | To fasten an item of clothing using a zip. |
| 7 | take off | To remove; the opposite of "put on". |
| 8 | wear out | To use an item so much that it becomes unfit for further use. |
