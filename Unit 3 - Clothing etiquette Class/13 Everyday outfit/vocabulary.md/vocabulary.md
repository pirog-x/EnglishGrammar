# Unit 3 Vocabulary

## Class 13: Everyday Outfit

### Class Profile

| Vocabulary | Can-do Statement |
| --- | --- |
| Nouns to describe clothing | Talk about clothing and describe the items people wear. |

| No. | Word / Phrase | Definition |
| ---: | --- | --- |
| 1 | blouse | A shirt, typically loose and reaching from the neck to the waist. |
| 2 | flip-flops | Sandals consisting of a rubber sole fastened to the foot by a rubber thong fitting between the toes and around the sides of the foot. |
| 3 | knitted sweater | A sweater which is made using knitting equipment. |
| 4 | heavy-duty boots | Boots designed for tough duties to help keep the wearer safe, agile, and comfortable. |
| 5 | high-rise jeans | Jeans with a fit at or above the belly button, generally with a rise measuring 9-10 inches. |
| 6 | low-rise jeans | A type of trousers that sit low on or below the hips. |
| 7 | overcoat | A heavy garment worn over other clothes for protection from cold or weather. |
| 8 | pencil skirt | A slim skirt with a straight and narrow cut, usually approximately knee-length. |
| 9 | pullover | A sweater that must be put on by pulling it over the head; a sweater without buttons or a zipper in front. |
| 10 | pump shoes | Women's shoes with a kitten or higher heel. |
| 11 | sneakers | Athletic shoes with a soft, rubber sole. |
| 12 | stiletto heels | Women's shoes with a thin, high, tapering heel. |
| 13 | tights | A close-fitting, sheer or non-sheer garment worn principally by women and girls that covers the body from the waist down, usually including the feet. |
| 14 | V-neck cardigan | A cardigan with a neckline that comes down to a point on the throat or chest, resembling the shape of the letter V. |
| 15 | wellington boots | Waterproof boots that cover the foot and lower part of the leg, usually reaching almost up to the knee. |
