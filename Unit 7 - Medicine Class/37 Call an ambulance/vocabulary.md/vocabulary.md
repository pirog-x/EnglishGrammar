# Unit 7 Vocabulary

## Class 37: Call an Ambulance!

### Class Profile

| Vocabulary | Can-do Statement |
| --- | --- |
| Health issues and symptoms | Talk about illnesses and describe symptoms. |

| No. | Word / Phrase | Definition |
| ---: | --- | --- |
| 1 | burn | Damage to your skin or body caused by heat, like a hot stove or fire. |
| 2 | blood pressure | The force of blood pushing against the walls of your blood vessels. |
| 3 | food poisoning | Getting sick from eating food that has gone bad or is contaminated with harmful germs. |
| 4 | stroke | A sudden problem with your brain that can affect your movement or speech. |
| 5 | heart attack | When your heart suddenly stops working well because of a blockage in your blood vessels. |
| 6 | flu | A contagious illness that gives you fever and aches, and makes you feel very tired. |
| 7 | inflammation | When a part of your body becomes red, swollen, and painful because it is fighting off germs or healing from an injury. |
| 8 | bleeding | When blood comes out of your body, like from a cut or injury. |
| 9 | choke | When something blocks your airway and makes it hard to breathe. |
| 10 | fever | When your body temperature is higher than usual because of an illness. |
| 11 | nausea | Feeling like you might throw up or having an upset stomach. |
| 12 | stuffy | When your nose feels blocked and you cannot breathe easily. |
| 13 | dizzy | Feeling like everything is spinning and you might lose your balance. |
| 14 | rash | Red, itchy spots on your skin that might be caused by an allergy or infection. |
| 15 | swelling | When a part of your body becomes larger than normal because of an injury or illness. |
