## First-aid Verb Phrases

| No. | Phrase | Definition |
| ---: | --- | --- |
| 1 | apply a tourniquet | Tie a band or device tightly above an injured area to stop severe bleeding temporarily. |
| 2 | apply direct pressure to the wound | Press firmly on a bleeding injury to slow or stop the bleeding. |
| 3 | cool the burn under cold running water | Rinse a burn with cold water to relieve pain and prevent further damage. |
| 4 | give an injection | Use a needle and syringe to deliver medication or fluids into the body. |
| 5 | perform CPR (cardiopulmonary resuscitation) | Use a life-saving technique involving chest compressions and rescue breaths to revive someone whose heart has stopped. |
| 6 | perform the Heimlich manoeuvre | Use a procedure to help a choking person by applying abdominal thrusts to dislodge a blockage. |
| 7 | put a cast | Encase a broken or injured body part in a hard, protective covering to aid in healing. |
| 8 | put a plaster on | Cover a small wound with an adhesive bandage to protect it. |
| 9 | put a sterile bandage | Apply a clean, germ-free dressing to a wound to prevent infection. |

## Medical Phrasal Verbs

| No. | Phrasal Verb | Definition |
| ---: | --- | --- |
| 1 | breathe in | To inhale air into your lungs. |
| 2 | breathe out | To exhale air from your lungs. |
| 3 | come down with | To become ill with a specific illness or condition. |
| 4 | come round | To become conscious again after an accident or medical operation. |
| 5 | get better | To recover from an illness or injury and return to good health. |
| 6 | pass away | To die. |
| 7 | pass out | To faint; lose consciousness for a short time. |
| 8 | throw up | To vomit. |
