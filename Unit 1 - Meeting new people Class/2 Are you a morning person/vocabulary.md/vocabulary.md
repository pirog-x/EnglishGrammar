### Class Profile

| Vocabulary | Can-do Statement |

| No. | Word / Phrase | Definition |
| ---: | --- | --- |

| 2 | foodie | A person with a particular interest in food. |
| 3 | homebody | A person who likes spending time at home rather than going out with friends. |
| 4 | movie buff | Someone who knows a lot about films. |
| 5 | cat or dog person | Someone who prefers cats or dogs or keeps them as pets. |
| 6 | early bird | A person who gets up or arrives early. |
| 7 | night owl | A person who prefers to be awake and active at night. |
| 8 | tech geek | Someone who knows a lot about science or technology, especially computers. |
| 9 | career-oriented | Focused on developing and succeeding in one's career. |
| 10 | family-oriented | Close to your family members and supportive of them. |
| 11 | be at my best | To be as active or intelligent as you can be. |
| 12 | be good at | Skilful and successful at doing something. |
| 13 | be indifferent to | Not thinking about or interested in someone or something. |
| 14 | be keen on | Very interested in, eager for, or wanting to do something very much. |
| 15 | be passionate about | Having or showing strong feelings about something. |
