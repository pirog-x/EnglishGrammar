# Unit 1 Vocabulary

## Class 1: Never Eat Alone!

### Class Profile

| Vocabulary | Can-do Statement |
| --- | --- |
| Verb phrases to speak about habits and behavior | Talk about my lifestyle, habits, and behavior. |

| No. | Verb Phrase | Definition |
| ---: | --- | --- |
| 1 | can't stand | To dislike or hate someone or something. |
| 2 | get on well | To have a friendly relationship. |
| 3 | hang out with sb | To spend a lot of time with someone. |
| 4 | have a guilty pleasure | To have an activity that you enjoy but would be embarrassed about if other people found out. |
| 5 | look up to sb | To admire and respect someone. |
| 6 | keep in touch | To continue to talk to or write to someone; to maintain contact. |
| 7 | keep things private | To maintain something in a secret or private state. |
| 8 | keep to yourself | To stay on your own most of the time and not mix socially with other people. |
| 9 | make up your mind | To make a decision; to decide. |
| 10 | socialise | To participate in social activities; to mix socially with others. |
