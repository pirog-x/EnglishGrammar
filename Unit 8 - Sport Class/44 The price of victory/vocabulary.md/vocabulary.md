## Sports Verb Phrases

|  No. | Phrase                            | Definition                                                                                 |
| ---: | --------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
|    1 | to bite the bullet                | To face a difficult or unpleasant situation with courage and determination.                |
|    2 | to come out on top                | To be successful in a competition or struggle.                                             |
|    3 | to face defeat                    | To accept losing in a competition or struggle.                                             |
|    4 | to face an uphill battle          | To encounter difficulties and obstacles that make achieving a goal challenging.            |
|    5 | to fall short                     | To fail to meet expectations or reach a desired outcome.                                   |
|    6 | to go for gold                    | To strive for the highest level of success or achievement.                                 |
|    7 | to miss the mark                  | To fail to achieve a goal or objective.                                                    |
|    8 | to play by the rules              | To follow what is generally held to be the correct line of behaviour.                      |
|    9 | to push yourself to the limit     | To give your absolute best and go beyond your comfort zone to achieve your full potential. |
|   10 | to pick up the pieces             | To try to recover from a difficult or traumatic experience.                                |
|   11 | to require blood, sweat and tears | To require a lot of hard work, effort, and determination.                                  |
|   12 | to seize the moment               | To take advantage of an opportunity when it presents itself.                               |
|   13 | to set goals                      | To establish specific objectives to work towards.                                          |
|   14 | to strive for something           | To work hard to achieve a specific goal.                                                   |
