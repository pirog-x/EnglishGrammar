# Unit 2 Vocabulary

## Class 8: Overcoming the Adaptation Stage

### Class Profile

| Vocabulary | Can-do Statement |
| --- | --- |
| Words and phrases to express one's emotions and feelings | Talk about one's emotions and feelings. |

| No. | Word / Phrase | Definition |
| ---: | --- | --- |
| 1 | to adjust to smth | To get used to a new situation by changing the way you behave and/or think. |
| 2 | to be charmed by | To feel a strong attraction or liking towards someone or something. |
| 3 | to become familiar with smth | To learn about something and become knowledgeable or experienced in it through practice or study. |
| 4 | to embrace the culture / rule | To fully accept and appreciate the customs, traditions, and way of life of a particular group or society. |
| 5 | to experience a feeling of euphoria | To feel an intense sense of happiness, excitement, and joy. |
| 6 | to find one's way | To get somewhere you are trying to reach. |
| 7 | to settle in somewhere | To become comfortable and established in a new place, such as a home or community. |
| 8 | at ease | Relaxed. |
| 9 | confused | Unable to think clearly or understand something. |
| 10 | disappointed | Unhappy because someone or something was not as good as you hoped or expected. |
| 11 | homesick | Sad, lonely, and nostalgic for one's home or country when away from it. |
| 12 | miserable | Very unhappy. |
| 13 | over the moon | Very pleased. |
| 14 | overwhelmed | So emotional that it is difficult to know how to react. |
